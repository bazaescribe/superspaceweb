## Information we handle

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, role, and message. This website does not currently provide an account registration, payment, or inquiry form.

We do not ask you to send sensitive personal information through this website. Please do not include passwords, financial details, health information, or confidential customer records in an initial inquiry.

## Why we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to you, understand your request, discuss whether Superspace fits your needs, and maintain the correspondence needed for that conversation. We also use necessary technical information to operate the website, troubleshoot problems, and protect against misuse.

An inquiry is not a subscription to a marketing list. If we introduce optional marketing communications or a materially different use of personal information, we will explain that use and provide the choices required by applicable law.

Hosting, email, and other providers supporting our website and communications may process information to provide those services. Their infrastructure may be located in other countries. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to address a lawful request.

If you follow a link to LinkedIn, another social network, or a scheduling provider, that provider handles information under its own privacy notice. Our website notice does not replace those notices.

## Cookies and measurement

The current website application does not set advertising cookies or load a third-party marketing analytics tracker. Hosting infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website. If we add analytics, embedded booking, or other tracking technology, we will update this notice and provide any required information and choices before using it.

## Retention and security

We keep personal information for the time needed to handle the inquiry or relationship, maintain appropriate business records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the nature of the correspondence and any applicable recordkeeping requirements.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle. No website, email service, or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

## Your requests and choices

You can contact jl@superspaceos.com to request access to, correction of, cancellation of, or opposition to the handling of your personal information (ARCO rights), to withdraw consent where applicable, or to limit use or disclosure. Include your name, a way to respond, the information concerned, and a clear description of your request. For a correction, describe the change and any supporting information.

We may need to verify your identity or your authority to act for someone else before completing a request. We will explain a suitable verification method; please do not send identity documents unsolicited. We will respond within the periods established by applicable law and explain any legal reason that prevents us from fulfilling a request. Withdrawing consent does not undo prior lawful processing, and some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.
